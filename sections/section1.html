Welcome & Course Overview

Welcome. It's a rare thing to pause and reflect on how we learn, and how others do too. This course offers that opportunity: a space for noticing, rethinking, and refining how we recognize and support learning differences in real classrooms.

This is not a course about diagnosing students. It's about noticing patterns. About learning how to observe carefully, document clearly, and respond thoughtfully. You'll engage with short readings, interactive activities, and guided reflections that highlight real-world situations faced by educators and students alike.

You'll explore how to:

  • Recognize early signs that a student may benefit from additional support
  • Document learning behaviors objectively and use strengths-based language
  • Collaborate with families, caregivers, and professionals
  • Develop thoughtful, inclusive strategies that center students—not labels

Most importantly, you'll meet students—not as diagnoses, but as people. There is no test at the end of this course. No final judgment. Just an invitation: to observe more closely, and carry that insight into your teaching practice.

Let's begin.
