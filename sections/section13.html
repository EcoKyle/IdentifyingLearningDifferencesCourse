Reflection

Pausing at the Threshold

Before we enter the final space of this course, let's pause at the threshold. This is the moment to look back at the corridors we've walked—through observation, interpretation, communication, and support. Each door opened a new way of seeing students, and perhaps ourselves.

Rather than rushing forward, this is your invitation to sit for a moment and gather what you've learned. No pressure to summarize it all—just enough to carry with you into the final step.

Optional Reflection Prompt

Choose one (or more) of the following prompts to reflect on. You may write your thoughts in a journal, speak aloud to yourself, or simply think quietly.

  • Which idea or phrase from this course keeps echoing in your mind?
  • What surprised you about your own perspective?
  • How has your sense of what it means to support a learner changed?
  • If you could offer one piece of advice to your past self at the start of this course, what would it be?

Looking Ahead
